How SkillSwap Works

Four simple steps to start learning and teaching among your college peers.

01
📝

Create Your Profile

Sign up with your college email. Add skills you can teach and skills you want to learn.

02
🔍

Discover Peers

Browse skill listings from students at your college. Filter by category, availability or rating.

03
🤝

Propose a Swap

Send a swap request — offer one of your skills in exchange for the skill you want to learn.

04
⭐

Learn & Rate

Meet up or connect online. Complete the swap and leave a review to build your reputation.
